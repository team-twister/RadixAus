Not for safety decisions. RadixAus is an independent radar app, not an official warning service. Radar here can be late, missing, misaligned or wrong. For warnings, always use the Bureau of Meteorology's official warnings and your state emergency service. In an emergency call 000. For storm or flood help call the SES on 132 500.

Who made this

RadixAus is a free storm-chasing radar made by Team Twister. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Bureau of Meteorology, the Commonwealth of Australia, NCI Australia, RainViewer, Esri, or the makers of RadarScope or GR2Analyst. Those names are used only to describe data sources and display styles.

Where the data comes from

Live radar (Live HD)

Reflectivity map tiles and Doppler wind images from the Bureau of Meteorology. © Commonwealth of Australia, Bureau of Meteorology.

Your phone loads these pictures directly from BoM's own servers each time you look. This site does not copy, store, change or sell them. See the BoM copyright terms. If BoM asks, the live radar will be removed or changed.

Weather History and the 3D storm pages

Radar volumes from the Australian Unified Radar Archive (AURA) Level 1 data, Bureau of Meteorology, hosted by NCI Australia.

Bureau of Meteorology, © Commonwealth of Australia. Licensed from the Commonwealth of Australia under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence. This app changes the data: it regrids the scans and draws them as 3D blocks, shells and volume renders. These changes are Team Twister's, not BoM's.

3D stack (stack.html)

Reflectivity from BoM where available, otherwise from the RainViewer API (© RainViewer). The live radar also switches to RainViewer by itself if BoM stops answering.

Weather warnings

Current warnings from the Bureau of Meteorology's weather website feed. © Commonwealth of Australia, Bureau of Meteorology. Thunderstorm areas on the map are drawn by this app around the towns each warning names, so they're approximate and not BoM's official warning areas.

Satellite before and after, damage overlay

Harmonized Landsat and Sentinel-2 (HLS) imagery and OPERA land surface disturbance alerts from NASA, via NASA GIBS, part of NASA's Earth Science Data and Information System (ESDIS). Landsat imagery courtesy of NASA and the U.S. Geological Survey; Sentinel-2 contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data, processed by ESA and NASA.

Maps

Dark base map © Bureau of Meteorology. Satellite imagery and place names © Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics and the GIS User Community.

Leaflet (BSD 2-Clause), three.js (MIT), MapLibre GL JS (BSD 3-Clause), h5wasm and the HDF5 library, each under its own open-source licence.

Privacy

No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and nothing you do is sent to Team Twister. Settings such as your chosen radar are saved only on your own phone. My location is used only on your phone to centre the map and pick the nearest radar. Like any map, your phone asks BoM, NCI, Esri and the other sources above for the images of the area you're viewing.

Contact and takedown

Questions, corrections, or a rights holder who wants something changed or removed: contact Team Twister on TikTok at @team.twister.official. Requests from data owners will be acted on promptly.
